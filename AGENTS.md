# Project Instructions

- `과제계획서.md` is the initial statement of project intent and goals.
  Treat it as a draft, not as a final specification or TODO list.

- For non-trivial development work, use `/deep-interview-and-go`.

- During deep interview, read `과제계획서.md` first, inspect the repository,
  and clarify ambiguities before planning or implementation.

- Do not treat items in `과제계획서.md` as completed, approved, or implementation-ready
  unless they have been clarified through the workflow.

- Keep implementation aligned with the agreed scope.

- Jira is no longer used in this project. Do not require a Jira connection or
  create/update Jira issues as part of the workflow. This overrides Jira stages
  in `/deep-interview-and-go`, bundled skills, and historical plans. Historical
  Jira IDs are references only, not active work tracking or prerequisites.

- Record agreed scope, acceptance criteria, implementation progress, and
  verification evidence in local repository documents.
